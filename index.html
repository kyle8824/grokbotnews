Persian Gulf

U.S. destroys five Iranian oil tankers after warship attacks; Iran fires on Jordan base as Brent tops $100

NEW · Wed. 8 a.m. EDT

U.S. Central Command said forces destroyed five IRGC-linked crude carriers on Sept. 8 — Kaviz, Charminar, Horizon 1, and Riesco in the Gulf of Oman and Derya near Kharg Island — after the IRGC twice targeted a U.S. Navy warship with ballistic missiles. The ship evaded the attacks; no U.S. personnel were harmed, and crews were told to abandon the tankers first. Iran’s IRGC claimed missile strikes on Al Azraq in Jordan and on vessels in Hormuz; Jordan said 18 of 20 projectiles were intercepted, two fell in unpopulated areas, and there were no casualties. Brent crude printed about $100.07 — the first close above $100 since July 24 — with WTI near $94.73, Reuters reported Wednesday.

Miami

NTSB recovers flight recorders after Miami Amazon cargo overrun; 5 dead on ground

Amazon Air Flight 7598, a Boeing 767 operated by 21 Air, overran a Miami International Airport runway around 1:53 p.m. Sunday after arriving from San Juan, Puerto Rico. The jet struck a cleaning-crew van with seven people aboard inside the airport fence and a Toyota outside; all five people killed were in the vehicles, and the flight crew survived. At a Monday briefing, NTSB Chair Jennifer Homendy said the flight data and cockpit voice recorders had been recovered and sent to NTSB headquarters in Washington, with 32 investigators on a “devastating” scene still in fact-finding. Two of four runways remained closed, fuel removal was ongoing, and the crew had not yet been interviewed.

Sources: BBC · Guardian · CBS Miami

Massachusetts

Judge declares mistrial in Lindsay Clancy triple-murder case after hung jury

Judge William Sullivan declared a mistrial Sept. 4 in Plymouth Superior Court after jurors reported they remained deadlocked following about seven days of deliberations in the murder trial of Lindsay Clancy, charged in the 2023 deaths of her children Cora, Dawson, and Callan. Reporting described an 11-1 split; the defense argued postpartum psychosis, and prosecutors said she knew right from wrong. Plymouth County District Attorney Tim Cruz has not decided whether to retry; a status conference is set for Sept. 29. Clancy remains held at Tewksbury State Hospital.

Center-left view

The 19th News, WBUR, PBS NewsHour, and BBC coverage emphasize the postpartum-psychosis defense and not-criminally-responsible argument, women rallying outside court in pink, and maternal mental-health system failures that supporters say left Clancy seeking care yet still overmedicated and untreated before the killings.

Sources: The 19th News · WBUR · PBS NewsHour · BBC

Center-right view

WCVB, CBS Boston, Fox News, and BBC accountability voices stress that three children were murdered; DA Tim Cruz says the case is about justice for Cora, Dawson, and Callan; the investigation did not establish depression caused the killings; first-degree charges were warranted; prosecutors said she knew right from wrong; critics reject sympathy as an excuse.

Justice Department

DOJ issues new Florida grand jury subpoenas in probe of Trump-Russia investigators

The Associated Press reported that the Justice Department is demanding testimony before a Florida grand jury in an investigation pursuing a theory that intelligence-community officials who scrutinized Donald Trump — including over Russian interference in the 2016 election — conspired against him and violated his rights. New subpoenas to former government officials mark an escalation in a year-long inquiry; Joe diGenova is counseling the attorney general on the effort. It was not immediately clear how many witnesses had been served or whether charges will follow.

Ohio

Armed man arrested after pushing toward Ohio gov candidate Amy Acton at Canfield Fair

Patrick Havas, 38, of Canfield was arrested Sunday inside a Democratic Party tent at the Canfield Fair after pushing through a crowd while gubernatorial candidate Amy Acton was speaking, according to the Mahoning County Sheriff’s Office and Ohio State Highway Patrol. Two people were knocked down; Acton was not injured. Deputies recovered two handguns and brass knuckles; investigators said no weapons were brandished or removed from holsters. Havas was booked on assault and disorderly conduct counts. Gov. Mike DeWine called violence or its threat at political events unacceptable.

Homeland Security

DHS secretary says ICE will not patrol polls but could enter for threats or warrants

Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin told reporters in New York that ICE “isn’t there to patrol the polling places” but could be present if there is a threat to a site or to serve a warrant on someone agents are actively tracking. A senior DHS official told NBC News that ICE officers have not received guidance to do anything beyond regular duties on Election Day. Democrats and voting-rights groups warn that federal agents at polls could intimidate voters; Mullin and DHS say any presence would be limited to specific threats or warrant service, not election patrols.

Trade

Trump moves to ban Canadian alcohol, dairy, and motorcycles as Ottawa’s counter-tariffs take effect

Canada’s counter-tariffs on roughly $20–28 billion of U.S. goods took effect at 12:01 a.m. ET Tuesday. Tuesday night President Trump signed proclamations to ban certain Canadian alcoholic beverages, dairy products, and motorcycles and mopeds from importation effective Sept. 29; additional Canadian goods face up to 50% tariffs, and GSA/USTR were directed to remove Canadian products from federal procurement schedules. Prime Minister Mark Carney said a pivot “will come at a cost.” USTR Ambassador Jamieson Greer framed the bans as a response to Canada’s retaliation after talks stalled.

Houthi missiles and drones hit Saudi energy sites; 73 wounded as oil moves through $100
