Miami

Amazon cargo jet overruns Miami runway; 5 dead, 5 hurt; NTSB opens probe

UPDATED · Mon. morning EDT

An Amazon Air Boeing 767-300 freighter operated by 21 Air as Flight 7598 overran a runway at Miami International Airport around 2 p.m. Sunday after arriving from San Juan, Puerto Rico, struck vehicles on a perimeter road and caught fire. Miami-Dade Mayor Daniella Levine Cava said five people died and five were injured, three critically. The FAA ordered a ground stop; FlightAware tracked more than 160 cancellations and about 325 delays. NTSB Chair Jennifer Homendy led a go-team to Miami with a Monday briefing planned. Amazon spokesperson Kelly Nantel offered condolences and said the company would cooperate with investigators.

Midterms

Missouri Supreme Court blocks Trump-backed House map for November

The Missouri Supreme Court unanimously ruled that the 2025 congressional map (HB 1) cannot be used in the Nov. 3 midterms unless voters first approve it in a referendum, and ordered Secretary of State Denny Hoskins to place the measure on the ballot. The court said the 2022 districts remain in force because a timely petition with more than 300,000 signatures suspended HB 1. Attorney General Catherine Hanaway said she would seek emergency relief at the U.S. Supreme Court; President Trump called the ruling “horrible, ridiculous, and unConstitutional.” The August primary used the 2025 lines; those primary results were not undone.

Center-left view

NBC News, Al Jazeera, the Missouri Independent, and Democracy Docket frame a unanimous state high-court block on a Trump-backed House map, a voter-driven referendum ordered onto the November ballot, and the return to 2022 districts for the general election.

Sources: NBC News · Al Jazeera · Missouri Independent · Democracy Docket

Center-right view

Postal Service

DOJ asks Supreme Court to clear USPS mail-ballot rule before midterms

The Justice Department asked the Supreme Court to stay U.S. District Judge Indira Talwani’s order blocking mandatory parts of the Postal Service’s Aug. 26 Ballot Mail for Federal Elections rule, seeking an administrative stay while states begin mailing. Solicitor General D. John Sauer argued North Carolina and Alabama start sending ballots as soon as Sept. 4 and that envelopes cannot be retrieved once mailed. Justice Jackson directed respondents to oppose by Sept. 8. Talwani held a hearing on extending the TRO; the portal for voter/barcode data is not yet operational.

Center-left view

NBC News, Reuters, SCOTUSblog, and CNN frame DOJ’s emergency Supreme Court ask against Talwani’s block, Purcell and midterm-mailing timing, and the still-dark ballot-mail portal as states prepare to send envelopes.

Sources: NBC News · Reuters · SCOTUSblog · CNN

Ukraine

Witkoff and Kushner meet Putin, then Zelensky; hope for trilateral talks

President Trump’s special envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met Russian President Vladimir Putin at the Kremlin on Saturday for more than three hours, then traveled by train to Kyiv on Sunday for their first wartime visit and talks with President Volodymyr Zelensky and European officials. A temporary no-shoot arrangement helped secure the travel window; Putin rejected a wider three-day frontline ceasefire while pausing aerial strikes on Kyiv briefly. In Kyiv, Witkoff said the delegation made progress in Moscow and hoped a trilateral meeting would be announced soon; national security advisers from the United Kingdom, France, and Germany joined sessions.

Sources: ABC News · Kyiv Post · Ukrinform

Persian Gulf

U.S. denies Iran struck uncrewed ship; Tehran plans Hormuz exclusion zone; oil near $97

CENTCOM spokesperson Capt. Energy

U.S. Labor Day gas hits record average near $4.14 as Hormuz risk persists

AAA said the national average for regular gasoline was about $4.14 a gallon heading into Labor Day, nearly a dollar higher than a year earlier and above the prior holiday-weekend record of $3.82 from 2012. Diesel averaged about $5.85, described as a record. Analysts and officials pointed to Iran-related Hormuz disruption plus high U.S. refinery utilization; Energy Secretary Chris Wright said markets still forecast lower prices in coming months while acknowledging prices are higher than Labor Day 2025.

Center-left view

Local AAA roundups and consumer desks tie the holiday record pump average to the Iran fight, Hormuz flows, and household cost-of-living pressure into the midterms.

Sources: ClickOrlando / AAA · Zee Business oil · AP Iran denial context

Center-right view

Sources: ClickOrlando (Wright remarks) · FXEmpire · Zee Business
