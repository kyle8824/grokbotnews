Miami

Amazon cargo jet overruns Miami runway; 5 dead, 5 hurt; NTSB probes late landing

UPDATED · Mon. afternoon EDT

An Amazon Air Boeing 767-300 freighter operated by 21 Air as Flight 7598 overran a Miami International Airport runway around 2 p.m. Sunday after arriving from San Juan, Puerto Rico, struck vehicles on a perimeter road and caught fire. Miami-Dade officials said five people died and five were injured, three critically; the FAA ordered a ground stop with more than 160 cancellations and about 325 delays before partial reopening. On Monday, NTSB Chair Jennifer Homendy led a go-team into Miami with a briefing planned around 4 p.m. ET as investigators and aviation experts examined whether the jet touched down too far down the runway to stop, why a go-around was not flown, weather and braking, and the aircraft’s maintenance history, including prior foreign ownership.

Midterms

Missouri Supreme Court blocks Trump-backed House map for November

The Missouri Supreme Court unanimously ruled that the 2025 congressional map (HB 1) cannot be used in the Nov. 3 midterms unless voters first approve it in a referendum, and ordered Secretary of State Denny Hoskins to place the measure on the ballot. The court said the 2022 districts remain in force because a timely petition with more than 300,000 signatures suspended HB 1. Attorney General Catherine Hanaway said she would seek emergency relief at the U.S. Supreme Court; President Trump called the ruling “horrible, ridiculous, and unConstitutional.” The August primary used the 2025 lines; those primary results were not undone.

Postal Service

DOJ renews Supreme Court ask after judge blocks USPS mail-ballot rule

U.S. District Judge Indira Talwani issued a Sept. 4 preliminary injunction blocking mandatory parts of the Postal Service’s Aug. 26 Ballot Mail for Federal Elections rule for the Nov. 3 midterms, replacing her earlier temporary restraining order. On Sunday the Justice Department withdrew its Sept. 3 Supreme Court application aimed at the TRO and filed a new emergency stay application against the preliminary injunction, with Solicitor General D. John Sauer citing North Carolina already mailing and Alabama set for Sept. 9. Justice Ketanji Brown Jackson directed challengers to respond by Wednesday, Sept. 9.

Ukraine

Witkoff and Kushner meet Putin, then Zelensky; hope for trilateral talks

President Trump’s special envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met Russian President Vladimir Putin at the Kremlin on Saturday for more than three hours, then traveled by train to Kyiv on Sunday for their first wartime visit and talks with President Volodymyr Zelensky and European officials. A temporary three-day reciprocal pause on strikes against each other’s capitals helped open a travel window; Putin rejected a wider frontline ceasefire. A Ukrainian presidential official told AFP the proposals were “more effective”; the White House said Moscow talks covered substantive plans to be announced in coming weeks. In Kyiv, Witkoff said the delegation made progress and hoped a trilateral meeting would be announced soon; E3 national security advisers from the United Kingdom, France, and Germany joined sessions. There was still no ceasefire breakthrough.

Persian Gulf

Iran plans Hormuz exclusion zone as oil nears $97; U.S. denies ship-strike claim

Energy

U.S. Labor Day gas hits record average near $4.14 as Hormuz risk persists

AAA said the national average for regular gasoline was about $4.14 a gallon heading into Labor Day, nearly a dollar higher than a year earlier and above the prior holiday-weekend record of $3.82 from 2012. Diesel averaged about $5.85, described as a record. Analysts and officials pointed to Iran-related Hormuz disruption plus high U.S. refinery utilization; Energy Secretary Chris Wright said about 9 million barrels a day were still getting through Hormuz — roughly two-thirds of pre-conflict flows with pipelines — and that markets still forecast lower prices in coming months while acknowledging prices are higher than Labor Day 2025.

Center-left view

Sources: ClickOrlando / AAA · Zee Business oil · AP Iran denial context
