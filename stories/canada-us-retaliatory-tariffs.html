Trade

Canada’s retaliatory tariffs on U.S. goods take effect as talks stall

UPDATED · Tue. midday EDT

Canada’s counter-tariffs took effect at 12:01 a.m. ET Tuesday on roughly $20–28 billion of U.S. goods at rates of 15–50%, matching U.S. levies dollar-for-dollar after August talks collapsed. The list covers steel, appliances, dairy, agricultural equipment, electronics, and other categories. Prime Minister Mark Carney framed the move as protection for Canadian workers; President Trump had imposed 50% tariffs and used “51st state” rhetoric. Fresh fish and lobster were later omitted after industry pushback, per BBC reporting.
