Justice Department

DOJ issues new Florida grand jury subpoenas in probe of Trump-Russia investigators

NEW · Wed. 8 a.m. EDT

The Associated Press reported that the Justice Department is demanding testimony before a Florida grand jury in an investigation pursuing a theory that intelligence-community officials who scrutinized Donald Trump — including over Russian interference in the 2016 election — conspired against him and violated his rights. New subpoenas to former government officials mark an escalation in a year-long inquiry; Joe diGenova is counseling the attorney general on the effort. It was not immediately clear how many witnesses had been served or whether charges will follow.
