Search for Smith after Grand Canyon flood still a rescue, not recovery

UPDATED · Thu. evening EDT

As of Thursday afternoon, NPS still called the hunt for Timothy Allen Smith, 53, of Star Valley Ranch, Wyoming, a search-and-rescue — not recovery — five days after the Aug. 29 Bright Angel Canyon flash flood. Giusti and Rosenkranz of Granbury, Texas, were identified earlier. BORSTAR, drones, K-9s continue; family traveled to Texas while search continues.
