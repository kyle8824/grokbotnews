Russia launches 453 drones overnight; Ukraine hits Tatarstan and Krasnodar oil sites

NEW · Sun. 9:15 a.m. EDT

Ukraine’s Armed Forces said Russia launched 453 drones overnight into Sunday, with 409 neutralized and strikes reported at 13 locations, including damage in Stryi (Lviv region) near a residential building, school outbuilding, and railway area. Separately, Russian authorities said a Ukrainian drone attack killed two and injured 12 at an industrial facility in Nizhnekamsk, Tatarstan; Ukraine’s Defence Intelligence said it also struck the Slavyansk-ECO oil refinery in Krasnodar Krai. The Guardian’s Sunday briefing tallied nine killed and dozens wounded in Saturday strikes as Kyiv warns of a hard winter. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said a Zelenskyy–Putin meeting on the margins of the Miami G20 was “impossible,” insisting any meeting be in Moscow.

Center-left view

Euronews, The Guardian, and AP photography context emphasize civilian and infrastructure damage from Russia’s 450-plus-drone overnight wave — 13 hit locations, Lviv-region Stryi damage near housing and rail, Kharkiv injuries — and Zelenskyy’s warning that Russia aims to “kill the Ukrainian economy” ahead of winter, while Kyiv continues long-range strikes on Russian oil and petrochemical sites.

Sources: Euronews · The Guardian · AP Photo (via Euronews)

Center-right view

Euronews and Russian regional statements foreground Ukraine’s overnight hits on Nizhnekamsk’s oil/petrochemical hub (two dead, 12 hurt per Russian authorities) and the claimed Slavyansk-ECO refinery strike in Krasnodar, while Peskov ruled out a G20-sidelines Putin–Zelenskyy meeting and Lavrov said Russia would not pause its Ukraine operation during possible talks — casting the energy duel as mutual winter preparation rather than a one-sided Russian air campaign.

Sources: Euronews (Tatarstan / Krasnodar) · The Guardian (G20 / winter) · Euronews (Peskov / Lavrov)
