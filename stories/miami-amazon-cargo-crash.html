Miami

Amazon cargo jet overruns Miami runway; 5 dead, 5 hurt; NTSB opens probe

UPDATED · Mon. morning EDT

An Amazon Air Boeing 767-300 freighter operated by 21 Air as Flight 7598 overran a runway at Miami International Airport around 2 p.m. Sunday after arriving from San Juan, Puerto Rico, struck vehicles on a perimeter road and caught fire. Miami-Dade Mayor Daniella Levine Cava said five people died and five were injured, three critically. The FAA ordered a ground stop; FlightAware tracked more than 160 cancellations and about 325 delays. NTSB Chair Jennifer Homendy led a go-team to Miami with a Monday briefing planned. Amazon spokesperson Kelly Nantel offered condolences and said the company would cooperate with investigators.
