UKMTO: unknown projectiles hit two vessels off Oman; IRGC claims Saildrone strike at Hormuz

NEW · Fri. 4:45 p.m. EDT

The UK Maritime Trade Operations center reported that four unknown projectiles struck two vessels about four nautical miles west of Khasab, Oman, near the Strait of Hormuz entrance late Thursday, with a fire on one ship and the second vessel’s status unclear (Warning 133-26). Separately, Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said its navy struck a U.S. Saildrone Explorer unmanned surface vessel (hull 5838) at the strait’s entrance, claiming it thwarted an “aggressive mission”; Washington had not confirmed the claim as of Friday reporting. Anadolu Agency, Ship & Bunker, Al Jazeera, and Xinhua carried the dual strands as Hormuz traffic remains depressed and Brent holds above $100.
