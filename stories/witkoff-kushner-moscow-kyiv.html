Ukraine

Russia resumes Kyiv strikes after U.S. envoys leave; capital pause ends

UPDATED · Tue. morning EDT

After Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner’s Moscow then Kyiv weekend talks, the three-day reciprocal pause on strikes against each other’s capitals expired Monday evening local time. Early Tuesday Russia hit Kyiv with ballistic missiles; Mayor Vitali Klitschko reported injuries and trapped residents, and Poland began air policing. The envoys had said talks were substantive with hope for trilateral talks; there was still no ceasefire breakthrough.
