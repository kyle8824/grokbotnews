Ukraine

Trump sends Witkoff and Kushner to Moscow, then Kyiv

UPDATED · Fri. 4:00 p.m. EDT

President Trump said Friday he sent special envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner to Moscow and Kyiv “to see whether or not we can get something done,” calling them “two great negotiators” and saying “there may be a good chance” of progress. TASS, citing a source, said the pair expect to arrive in Moscow then Kyiv on Sept. 5–6; Zelensky confirmed a Sept. 6 Kyiv meeting after Moscow and pledged no Ukrainian airstrikes during the visit while urging Russia to match a ceasefire. Kyiv Independent reported the Kyiv stop had been in doubt amid Russian drone barrages and sources saying Witkoff was wary of the security picture; Washington Examiner and TASS framed a Kremlin stop before Kyiv as the path to restart talks.

Sources: CNN · Kyiv Independent · Kyiv Post
