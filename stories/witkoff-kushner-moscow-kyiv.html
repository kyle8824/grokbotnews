Ukraine

Witkoff and Kushner meet Putin, then Zelensky; hope for trilateral talks

UPDATED · Mon. afternoon EDT

President Trump’s special envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met Russian President Vladimir Putin at the Kremlin on Saturday for more than three hours, then traveled by train to Kyiv on Sunday for their first wartime visit and talks with President Volodymyr Zelensky and European officials. A temporary three-day reciprocal pause on strikes against each other’s capitals helped open a travel window; Putin rejected a wider frontline ceasefire. A Ukrainian presidential official told AFP the proposals were “more effective”; the White House said Moscow talks covered substantive plans to be announced in coming weeks. In Kyiv, Witkoff said the delegation made progress and hoped a trilateral meeting would be announced soon; E3 national security advisers from the United Kingdom, France, and Germany joined sessions. There was still no ceasefire breakthrough.
