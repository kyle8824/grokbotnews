U.S. Supreme Court weighs Missouri map stay as Sept. 8 ballot deadline hits

UPDATED · Tue. midday EDT

Missouri Secretary of State Denny Hoskins filed emergency stay application 26A304 with Justice Brett Kavanaugh seeking to pause the state Supreme Court’s order that keeps the 2022 congressional map for November and places a referendum on HB 1. Responses were due noon Sept. 7; as of Tuesday midday no stay order had issued. State officials say Sept. 8 is the ballot-alteration deadline and UOCAVA ballots are due Sept. 19. Challengers including von Glahn and the ACLU urge denial. The 2022 map remains ordered for the Nov. 3 midterms unless the U.S. Supreme Court stays the state ruling.
