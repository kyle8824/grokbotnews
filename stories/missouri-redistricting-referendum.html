Midterms

Missouri Supreme Court blocks Trump-backed House map for November

UPDATED · Thu. evening EDT

The Missouri Supreme Court unanimously ruled Thursday that the 2025 congressional map (HB 1) cannot be used in the Nov. 3 midterms unless voters first approve it in a referendum, and ordered Secretary of State Denny Hoskins to place the referendum on the ballot by Sept. 8. The court said the 2022 districts remain in force because a timely petition with more than 300,000 signatures suspended HB 1. Attorney General Catherine Hanaway said she would appeal to the U.S. Supreme Court; President Trump called the ruling “horrible, ridiculous, and unConstitutional.” The August primary used the 2025 lines; the court did not undo those primary results.
