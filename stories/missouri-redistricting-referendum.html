Missouri ballot-alteration deadline arrives as U.S. Supreme Court still weighs map stay

UPDATED · Tue. 4 p.m. EDT

Missouri Secretary of State Denny Hoskins’s emergency stay application 26A304 before Justice Brett Kavanaugh remained pending Tuesday afternoon with no ruling posted on the docket. Responses were due noon Sept. 7. Tuesday is Missouri’s Sept. 8 ballot-alteration deadline under state law; officials are also watching the UOCAVA mail deadline of Sept. 19. Challengers including von Glahn and the ACLU urge denial. The 2022 map remains ordered for the Nov. 3 midterms unless the U.S. Supreme Court stays the state ruling that blocked the 2025 map and set a referendum on HB 1.
