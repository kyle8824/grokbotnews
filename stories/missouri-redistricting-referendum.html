Kavanaugh denies Missouri map stay as federal judge orders 2025 lines for November

UPDATED · Wed. 8 a.m. EDT

Justice Brett Kavanaugh denied Secretary of State Denny Hoskins’s emergency stay application seeking to reinstate Missouri’s 2025 congressional map after the Missouri Supreme Court ordered the 2022 lines and a November referendum on HB 1. The same day, U.S. District Judge Stephen R.
