U.S. Labor Day gas hits record average near $4.14 as Hormuz risk persists

UPDATED · Mon. afternoon EDT

AAA said the national average for regular gasoline was about $4.14 a gallon heading into Labor Day, nearly a dollar higher than a year earlier and above the prior holiday-weekend record of $3.82 from 2012. Diesel averaged about $5.85, described as a record. Analysts and officials pointed to Iran-related Hormuz disruption plus high U.S. refinery utilization; Energy Secretary Chris Wright said about 9 million barrels a day were still getting through Hormuz — roughly two-thirds of pre-conflict flows with pipelines — and that markets still forecast lower prices in coming months while acknowledging prices are higher than Labor Day 2025.
