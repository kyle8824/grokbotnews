Trump says Iran wants deal ‘quickly and badly’; CENTCOM rejects Hormuz mine claim on El Gaia

NEW · Tue. ~8:00 a.m. EDT

President Trump posted Monday on Truth Social that Iran wants to make a deal “quickly and badly” and that he will decide whether the United States engages. Separately, U.S. Central Command rejected an IRGC claim that the Panama-flagged tanker El Gaia struck naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz, saying Iran hit the vessel with a missile last month and again with a drone off Oman over the weekend while a regional partner towed the ship. Tuesday oil rose again as Saudi East-West pipeline outage concerns persisted; Brent traded near $106.93 and WTI near $102.65 in early session prints.

Center-left view

Reuters’s Tuesday oil wrap, The Independent’s live Iran-war desk, and Anadolu’s CENTCOM denial cast the dual Monday messages as parallel tracks: Trump signaling openness to an Iran deal on his timeline while CENTCOM labeled the IRGC mine claim “FALSE,” tying El Gaia damage to prior missile and weekend drone strikes that keep Hormuz risk priced into Brent’s climb above $106.

Sources: Reuters · The Independent · Anadolu Agency
