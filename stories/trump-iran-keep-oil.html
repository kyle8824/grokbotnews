Trump says U.S. could stay in Iran and ‘keep the oil,’ citing Venezuela deal

NEW · Sun. 4:15 p.m. EDT

At the Irish Open in Doonbeg on Sunday, President Trump said the United States could “stay and keep the oil” in Iran, comparing that option to his administration’s Venezuela oil arrangement, and added that Venezuela revenue had “paid for the war many times.” He said the Iran war may end around or just after the November midterms, that gasoline would then “drop like a rock,” and that Iran was “calling constantly” for a deal — while insisting he would only accept the “right deal.”

Center-left view

Reuters, The Hill, and The Jerusalem Post lead with Trump’s Doonbeg line that the U.S. might “stay and keep the oil” in Iran like the Venezuela deal, his midterms-timed war-end forecast, and the claim that Tehran is “calling constantly” — alongside market context that Middle East turmoil and the Saudi pipeline outage already have oil traders bracing for a Monday bounce.

Sources: Reuters · The Hill · Jerusalem Post

Center-right view

Al Arabiya English, KVIA/ABC, and Arab News stress Trump’s Sunday forecast that the Iran war ends around or after the midterms with gasoline set to “drop like a rock,” his insistence he will only take the “right deal,” and the Venezuela parallel — including his claim that U.S. Venezuela oil revenue has “paid for the war many times” — as he spoke from the Irish Open at Doonbeg.

Sources: Al Arabiya English · KVIA / ABC · Arab News
