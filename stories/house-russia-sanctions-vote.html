House Rules meets Monday 4 p.m. on Graham Russia–Iran sanctions bill

UPDATED · Mon. 8 a.m. EDT

The House Rules Committee is scheduled to meet Monday at 4 p.m. Eastern in Capitol room H-313 on Senate amendments to H.R. 5334, the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 — a procedural step toward a possible floor vote later this week. The Senate passed the measure 86–11 on Aug. 7. The bill would authorize tariffs of up to 100% on the world’s top five buyers of Russian crude and natural gas, expand Russia/Iran-related sanctions, and faces Democratic concerns over expanded presidential tariff authority. House leaders have said this week may be the last practical floor window before midterm campaign travel.

Center-left view

The Hill, AP/ABC, and Monday agenda write-ups emphasize Democratic resistance to expanded presidential tariff authority even among members who want tougher Russia pressure, and note that a Rules meeting alone does not pass the bill — regular-order still needs near-unanimous House Republican support on the rule before any floor vote.

Sources: The Hill · ABC News / AP · 111 Things (Rules agenda)

Center-right view

Bloomberg’s scheduling report, the Washington Examiner’s Senate write-through, and Speaker Johnson’s bipartisan-consensus line frame Monday’s 4 p.m. Rules meeting as the next Trump-backed checkpoint after Graham’s 86–11 Senate win — authorizing secondary tariffs on top Russian energy buyers while casting House hesitation as the remaining obstacle.

Sources: Bloomberg · Washington Examiner · The Hill
