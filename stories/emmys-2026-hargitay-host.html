Mariska Hargitay to host 78th Emmys on NBC and Peacock Sept. 14

NEW · 5:36 p.m. EDT

The 78th Primetime Emmy Awards air live Monday, Sept. 14 at 8 p.m. ET from Los Angeles’ Peacock Theater, with Law & Order: SVU star Mariska Hargitay hosting, NBC and the Television Academy said. The Pitt leads nominations with 25; Hacks follows with 24. Hargitay already won two Creative Arts Emmys this month for her documentary My Mom Jayne. NBC marks its 100th anniversary with a peacock-blue carpet; Peacock streams live.
