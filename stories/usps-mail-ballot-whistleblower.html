Postal Service

DOJ renews Supreme Court ask after judge blocks USPS mail-ballot rule

UPDATED · Tue. midday EDT

U.S. District Judge Indira Talwani issued a Sept. 4 preliminary injunction blocking mandatory parts of the Postal Service’s Aug. 26 Ballot Mail for Federal Elections rule for the Nov. 3 midterms, replacing her earlier temporary restraining order. On Sunday the Justice Department withdrew its Sept. 3 Supreme Court application aimed at the TRO and filed a new emergency stay application (26A305) against the preliminary injunction, with Solicitor General D. John Sauer citing North Carolina already mailing and Alabama set for Sept. 9. Justice Ketanji Brown Jackson directed challengers to respond by 4 p.m. EDT Wednesday, Sept. 9.
