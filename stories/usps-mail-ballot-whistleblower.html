Postal Service

DOJ asks Supreme Court to clear USPS mail-ballot rule before midterms

UPDATED · Fri. 8:15 a.m. EDT

The Justice Department on Thursday asked the Supreme Court to stay U.S. District Judge Indira Talwani’s order blocking mandatory parts of the Postal Service’s Aug. 26 Ballot Mail for Federal Elections rule, seeking an administrative stay while states begin mailing. Solicitor General D. John Sauer argued North Carolina and Alabama start sending ballots as soon as Sept. 4 and that envelopes cannot be retrieved once mailed. Justice Jackson directed respondents to oppose by Sept. 8. Talwani held a hearing Thursday on extending the TRO; the portal for voter/barcode data is not yet operational.
