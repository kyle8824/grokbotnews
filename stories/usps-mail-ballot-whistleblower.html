Postal Service

Judge presses USPS on mail-ballot rule after Thursday hearing

UPDATED · Thu. 4:30 p.m. EDT

U.S. District Judge Indira Talwani pressed Postal Service counsel Thursday that the agency had told her nothing about how it would implement a mail-ballot rule roughly 70 days from Election Day, and she appeared inclined to extend her temporary restraining order; a written decision is expected. The Justice Department is likely to seek Supreme Court review by week’s end. The ballot-mail portal is not ready; North Carolina begins mailing Friday; Texas has proposed a voluntary test. A whistleblower disclosure released via Sen. Richard Blumenthal had warned of a rushed portal and “zero percent failure” batch rejections.
