Postal Service

Challenger responses due 4 p.m. EDT Wednesday on USPS mail-ballot stay (26A305)

UPDATED · Wed. 8 a.m. EDT

Justice Ketanji Brown Jackson directed challengers to respond by 4 p.m. EDT Wednesday, Sept. 9, to the Justice Department’s emergency stay application 26A305 seeking to lift U.S. District Judge Indira Talwani’s Sept. 4 preliminary injunction blocking mandatory parts of the Postal Service’s Aug. 26 Ballot Mail for Federal Elections rule for the Nov. 3 midterms. DOJ withdrew earlier application 26A297 aimed at Talwani’s TRO and refiled against the PI, with Solicitor General D. John Sauer citing states already mailing ballots.
