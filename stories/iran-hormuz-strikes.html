Persian Gulf

Iran plans Hormuz exclusion zone as oil nears $99 on Houthi Saudi strikes; U.S. denial stands

UPDATED · Tue. 4 p.m. EDT

CENTCOM spokesperson Capt. Tim Hawkins on Monday called an Iranian claim that Tehran struck an uncrewed U.S. military vessel in the Strait of Hormuz a “total lie”; that denial still stood Tuesday afternoon. Iran’s Supreme National Security Council secretary Mohsen Rezaei said Tehran would announce a restricted/exclusion zone and Hormuz shipping corridor maps in the coming days, with an Oman corridor still in play. Same-day Houthi missile and drone strikes on Saudi energy sites added pressure; afternoon prints put Brent near $98.60–$99 and WTI near $93.70–$94 after session highs near $99.46 and $94.73. Energy Secretary Chris Wright had said about 9 million barrels a day were getting through — roughly two-thirds of pre-conflict flows with pipelines.
