Persian Gulf

Iran plans Hormuz exclusion zone as oil nears $97; U.S. denies ship-strike claim

UPDATED · Mon. afternoon EDT

CENTCOM spokesperson Capt. Tim Hawkins called an Iranian claim that Tehran struck an uncrewed U.S. military vessel in the Strait of Hormuz a “total lie.” Iran’s Supreme National Security Council secretary Mohsen Rezaei said Tehran would announce a restricted/exclusion zone and Hormuz shipping corridor maps in the coming days. Parliament speaker Mohammad Bagher Qalibaf warned that Gulf energy infrastructure, including U.S. interests, is exposed. Monday session prints put Brent near $97.07 and WTI near $92.28; commodity shipping through Hormuz ran about 10 vessels a day, the lowest since May. Energy Secretary Chris Wright said about 9 million barrels a day were getting through — roughly two-thirds of pre-conflict flows with pipelines.
