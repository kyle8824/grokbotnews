ENERGY

Iran’s Hormuz exclusion-zone plans remain as Brent clears $100 on wider Gulf fighting

UPDATED · Wed. 8 a.m. EDT

CENTCOM spokesperson Capt. Tim Hawkins’s Monday denial that Iran struck an uncrewed U.S. military vessel in the Strait of Hormuz — calling the claim a “total lie” — still stood Wednesday morning. Iran’s Supreme National Security Council secretary Mohsen Rezaei has said Tehran would announce a restricted/exclusion zone and Hormuz shipping corridor maps, with an Oman corridor still discussed. Overnight U.S. destruction of five Iranian oil tankers and Iran’s claimed Jordan-base fire shifted the energy story to Brent about $100.07 and WTI near $94.73; this item tracks the standing Hormuz-zone and denial context separate from the tanker lead.
