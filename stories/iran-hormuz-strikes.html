Persian Gulf

Iran plans Hormuz exclusion zone as oil holds near $97; U.S. denies ship-strike claim

UPDATED · Tue. midday EDT

CENTCOM spokesperson Capt. Tim Hawkins on Monday called an Iranian claim that Tehran struck an uncrewed U.S. military vessel in the Strait of Hormuz a “total lie”; that denial still stood Tuesday. Iran’s Supreme National Security Council secretary Mohsen Rezaei said Tehran would announce a restricted/exclusion zone and Hormuz shipping corridor maps in the coming days, with an Oman corridor still in play. Tuesday prints put Brent near $97.30 and WTI near $92.60. Energy Secretary Chris Wright had said about 9 million barrels a day were getting through — roughly two-thirds of pre-conflict flows with pipelines.
