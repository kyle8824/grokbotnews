Persian Gulf

Iran hits U.S. bases in Kuwait as Trump threatens more Hormuz strikes

UPDATED · Thu. 8:15 a.m. EDT

On Thursday morning, Kuwait’s army said it was confronting Iranian missile and drone attacks aimed at U.S. bases, a day after President Trump said U.S. forces staged a “very heavy attack” Tuesday night and are prepared to strike Iran “any time we want.” Iranian health officials said at least 18 people were killed and more than 140 wounded in U.S. strikes since Sunday, including a wedding party in Kuhestak that Iran’s Red Crescent asked the ICC to investigate. CENTCOM and U.S. officials said Tuesday’s wave hit nearly 60 IRGC targets while escorting about 40 commercial vessels carrying roughly 18 million barrels of oil through Hormuz. Trump said the renewed fighting will not last “too long” and claimed U.S. control of the strait; Tehran rejects that claim. White House aides, per Reuters pickups, want to keep the war relatively quiet before November’s midterms.
