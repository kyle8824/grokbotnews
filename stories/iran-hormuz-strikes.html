Persian Gulf

U.S. denies Iran struck uncrewed ship; Tehran plans Hormuz exclusion zone; oil near $97

UPDATED · Mon. morning EDT

CENTCOM spokesperson Capt. Tim Hawkins called an Iranian claim that Tehran struck an uncrewed U.S. military vessel in the Strait of Hormuz a “total lie.” Iran’s new Supreme National Security Council head said Tehran plans to announce an exclusion zone outside the strait aimed at vessels it believes are attempting transit. The denial followed weekend tit-for-tat strikes on vessels after a quieter stretch. Monday oil prints put WTI near $92.14 and Brent near $96.80 as Hormuz supply risk stayed in the market.
