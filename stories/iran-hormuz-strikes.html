Vance says Iran fight isn’t a “war” as EU joins economic pressure and oil nears $96

UPDATED · Fri. 8:15 a.m. EDT

Vice President JD Vance said Thursday he would not call the U.S. fight with Iran a “war,” telling reporters major combat had lasted about six weeks and “right now, there is no active shooting,” even as Iran claimed strikes this week on U.S. bases in Kuwait and the UAE and oil rose toward $96 on Hormuz risk. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the European Union officially joined Operation Economic Outcast. Vance said China is “willing to play ball” on isolating Tehran. Kuwait said it intercepted and condemned attacks on Ahmad al-Jaber; the UAE reported no Al Minhad strike. Vance said Washington is probing Iranian wedding-strike claims; CENTCOM has said U.S. forces do not target civilians.
