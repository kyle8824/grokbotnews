Persian Gulf

Iran says it hit U.S. bases in Kuwait and UAE as Trump threatens more strikes

UPDATED · Thu. 4:30 p.m. EDT

Iran’s army said Thursday it struck U.S. bases in Kuwait and the UAE — naming Ahmad al-Jaber Air Base in Kuwait and Al Minhad Air Base in the Emirates — as President Trump threatened more strikes and said U.S. forces can hit Iran “any time we want.” Kuwait said it was intercepting and condemned the attacks; the UAE reported no such attack, per France 24. Vice President Vance said he was investigating Iranian media claims of a wedding-party strike and expressed skepticism; CENTCOM’s Capt. Hawkins said U.S. forces never target civilians. Iranian health officials put recent U.S. strike deaths at about 18–19. Trump said renewed fighting will not last “too long” and claimed Hormuz control with roughly 18 million barrels escorted. Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu said Iran’s regime is teetering and weaker than ever.
