Google opens Dreambeans AI app to all U.S. users 18 and older

NEW · 3:39 p.m. EDT

Google’s Dreambeans AI app is rolling out to every U.S. account 18+, The Verge reported Thursday, after a June test. The Android and iOS app pulls from Search, Gmail, Photos, Calendar, and Gemini to surface shopping tips, travel ideas, and event reminders paired with storybook-style drawings of the user. Google positioned the launch as a consumer companion layered on existing Google apps rather than a workplace agent.
