Trump delivers Dallas midterm keynote; floats $5,000 ‘Trump dividend’ if GOP holds Congress

UPDATED · Wed. 10:30 p.m. EDT

President Donald Trump gave Wednesday night’s keynote at the Republican Party’s first national midterm convention at Dallas’s American Airlines Center, urging voters to “pretend” he is on the Nov. 3 ballot and promising a $5,000 “Trump dividend” to every adult U.S. citizen if Republicans keep the House and Senate, conditioned on spending the money in the United States. NBC News reported the White House did not immediately detail funding for the roughly $1.35 trillion idea. Trump also said the Iran war would end after Election Day, drew boos when he praised Senate Majority Leader John Thune, and again backed Texas AG Ken Paxton for Senate. Sen. John Fetterman (D-Pa.) appeared in a prerecorded video introducing Sen. Dave McCormick (R-Pa.). Trump returns Thursday after Vice President JD Vance’s keynote.

Center-left view

NBC News’s live blog, Reuters, and The Guardian stress unanswered questions on how a ~$1.35 trillion “Trump dividend” would be funded; Trump’s stretched Iran timeline (from weeks to post-election); empty upper-bowl seats despite a “packed” claim; crowd boos for John Thune; and NBC’s count that dozens of vulnerable Republicans skipped Dallas amid low approval and Iran/cost-of-living drag.

Sources: NBC News · Reuters · The Guardian

Center-right view

Fox News and CBS News highlight Fetterman’s surprise video as a “commonsense Democrat” rejecting socialism while praising McCormick and steel-country work with Trump; Fox’s RNC Chair Joe Gruters pitch that Trump is the best draw for low-propensity voters; and Trump’s on-stage turnout ask to treat midterms as if he is on the ballot while stumping for Paxton and vowing ~35 midterm races.

Sources: Fox News · Fox News · CBS News
